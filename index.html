Tap Dodge Rush

Live on Google Play Arcade Rated Everyone Free Contains ads

A fast, reflex-based arcade game built around one idea: dodge the red blocks for as long as you can. The rules take three seconds to learn and considerably longer to master — one mistake and it's over. Made for thirty-second sessions and just-one-more-try evenings.

About

SeraphLight Studios

SeraphLight Studios is an independent game studio based in Charleston, South Carolina, founded and run by Erik Hill. We make small, focused games for Android — the kind you can learn in seconds and keep coming back to.

Everything here is built in-house: the code, the art, and the tooling that tests it. Tap Dodge Rush shipped to Google Play in April 2026 after two weeks of closed testing. An unannounced match-3 RPG is in development now.

We keep the scope small on purpose. A finished game that works on an old phone beats an ambitious one that never ships.
